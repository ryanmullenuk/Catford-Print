What is bleed?

Bleed is the printed area that runs beyond the finished edge of your document and is trimmed away after printing.

Why bleed is needed

Commercial printing is cut to the finished size in stacks. A small amount of movement can occur when sheets are trimmed. Without bleed, this can leave an unwanted white line at the edge.

Add 3mm on every edge

If colour or an image is intended to reach the edge, extend it 3mm beyond the finished size. An A4 page finished at 210 × 297mm should therefore have artwork sized to 216 × 303mm.

Keep important content clear

Keep text, logos, page numbers and other important information at least 7–8mm inside the finished edge. Bleed is for background colours and images, not important content.

Supply a print-ready PDF

Export the finished document as a high-quality PDF. Cut marks are not necessary unless specifically requested.
