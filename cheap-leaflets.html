Terms

Production time is 5-7 working days from receipt of your final print ready PDF.

A 3 working day service is often available at an extra cost of £50.

Prices are based on just opening and printing your print ready PDF.

Any work we need to do to make your job print ready is chargeable.

Any work we need to do to change your file into a PDF is chargeable.

If the print is to go right to the edge of the sheet then 3mm bleed needs to be added.

Collection from our premises must be Monday - Friday 9.15am - 5pm.

Delivery is by Parcelforce and costs £12. They usually take 1-2 working days. UK Mainland only. Delivery costs to Highlands & Islands, N.Ireland, Channel Isles and IOM along with some other areas cost more and take longer. Please ask for a quote.

Leaflets are usually zero rated for VAT unless they are intended to be written on. If unsure please ask.
