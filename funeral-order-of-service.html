Step 5. Customise your chosen template

Take time with your family to customise your Funeral Order of Service template on your PC. It is essential that you show your design to the person conducting the service as they may have special requirements concerning the format.

The closest relative is the best one to decide on the final wording. Avoid involving other well meaning family members as this may only add to what is already a stressful time with unnecessary minor changes.
