Included in the total price is a set up charge of £40 and you will also receive an un-laminated but bound sample copy to make sure you are happy with what you have supplied us.

After receiving this, if you decide you want to make changes (no matter how small) we will have to start again and there will be a new set up charge of £40.

Therefore, if you haven't proof read your document properly before sending it to us then we

strongly suggest

you do so.

The further £40 charge will of course entitle you to receive a fresh sample.

The prices quoted here are PLUS VAT where applicable.

If your document is intended to be completed in some way by the reader then it will almost certainly be subject to VAT.

The possible exception to this will be if the document is a clearly defined school children's work book. Children being under 18.

This exception therefore doesn't include university or adult work works.
