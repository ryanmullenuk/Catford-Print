Info

Discounts:

The prices shown are for 30 day account customers. If you don't have an account we will need payment in advance (credit card, cheque, cash, BACS) for which we will allow a

5% discount.

Orders over £950 attract more discount.

Collection:

As these prices include delivery, a further

7% discount

is available if you can collect your work from our premises in SE6.

Delivery:

England, Wales, most areas of Scotland:

Orders over £100 are sent by ParcelForce 24hr service. Orders under £100 are sent by ParcelForce 48hr. Orders under £30 are subject to a small order delivery supplement of £3. Upgrades are available.

Scottish Highlands & Islands, N.Ireland, Channel Isles, IOM, Overseas etc.:

Please contact us.

Delivery is made Mon-Fri 8.30am - 6pm and the parcel will need a signature. Please note that ParcelForce

do not offer compensation for delayed parcels

and therefore neither can we unless you request our consequential loss insurance.

Hard Copy:

We cannot work from hard copy.

Electronic Files:

We can accept PDF's and most popular software applications by email (max. 10mb), disk or via our FTP site for large files. Look for the link on the left.

Margins:

Bleed is possible for documents supplied electronically but please provide this at 3mm. Cut marks are not necessary. If you do not require bleed then please make sure you keep text and important images no closer than 8mm from the edge of the sheet.

Solids:

Large areas of solid colour may not be possible for us to print - please contact us for a chat.

VAT:

CD & DVD Booklets are zero rated. All other CD & DVD printed material is subject to VAT.
