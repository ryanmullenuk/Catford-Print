Discounts:

The prices shown are for 30 day account customers. If you don't have an account we will need payment in advance (credit card, cheque, cash, BACS) for which we will allow a

5% discount.

Orders over £950 attract more discount.

VAT:

This is extra and all

posters

are subject to VAT.

Delivery:

England, Wales, most areas of Scotland:

ParcelForce 24hr service : £11 + VAT per consignment. ParcelForce 2-3 day service is £7 + VAT. We will try to get as many posters in one consignment as possible.

Scottish Highlands & Islands, N.Ireland, Channel Isles, IOM, Overseas etc.:

Please contact us.

Delivery is made Mon-Fri 8.30am - 6pm and the parcel will need a signature. Please note that ParcelForce

do not offer compensation for delayed parcels

and therefore neither can we unless you request our consequential loss insurance.

Indoor Use:

These posters must not be exposed to damp conditions or sun light. They are not suitable for hanging against windows etc. If hung on the wall away from sun light or direct illumination then they will last up to 2 years.

Encapsulation:

Encapsulated posters will have an additional sealed 10mm lip on all 4 edges. They are an ideal way of protecting the poster against dirty marks or creases - especially if the poster is to be handled regularly. The largest size we can encapsulate is A0.

Outdoor Use:

Encapsulated posters will provide

short-term shower proof protection

as long as the encapsulate isn't damaged. Encapsulated posters are not an alternative to long term laminated PVC signs from sign writers.

Electronic Files:

We can accept PDF's and most popular software applications by email (max. 10mb), disk or via our FTP site for large files. Look for the link above.

Resolution:

Few customers provide us with files at the correct size and ask that we enlarge them. Whilst this is no problem to us please study the chart about enlargement and pixelization.

Solids:

Our equipment is designed for printing photographic images. Large areas of solid colour may not be possible for us to print - please contact us for a chat.
