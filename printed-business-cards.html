** It is very important for us to tell you something ....

Prices are based on just opening and printing your file.

Any work we need to do to make your file print ready is chargeable.

Standard business cards are 85 x 55mm but other sizes are also available.

Designs should ensure that text or important data is at least 7mm away from the edge of the card.

If the print goes right to the edge of the card then 3mm bleed needs to be added.

If unsure please ask us.

Delivery by Royal Mail is usually 1-3 days after dispatch date.

Cards with large areas of solid will require the extra cost of lamination.

Lamination takes an extra day.

Double Thickness Luxury Cards take a little longer than Standard Rigid ones.
