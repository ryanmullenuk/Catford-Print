Preparing your book cover

A book cover is supplied as one flat piece containing the back cover, spine and front cover, with bleed around the outside.

Cover layout

Arrange the flat cover from left to right as back cover, spine and front cover. The total width is both cover widths plus the calculated spine width.

Spine width

The spine depends on the number of pages and the selected paper. Confirm the final paper and page count before finishing the cover artwork. The paperback quotation page provides an estimated spine.

Bleed and safe area

Add 3mm bleed around the complete outside edge. Keep titles, logos, barcodes and other important content at least 7–8mm inside all cut and fold lines.

Spine text

Allow clear space either side of spine wording. Very thin books may not have enough room for readable spine text.

PDF delivery

Supply the cover as a separate, print-ready PDF at the exact flat size. Supply the inner pages as a second PDF in normal reading order.
